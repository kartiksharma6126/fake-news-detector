    How the model decides

    // SPEC SHEET

    Model
    Logistic Regression
    Features
    TF-IDF, words + bigrams
    Vocabulary
    up to 50,000 terms
    Training split
    80% train / 20% test
    Dataset
    ISOT (Kaggle) or built-in sample
    Served by
    Flask + joblib
    1. 01

      Clean the text

      Lowercase it, strip URLs, punctuation and source tags like “(Reuters)”, then drop common stopwords. This removes shortcuts so the model can’t just memorize which news agency wrote a story.

    2. 02

      Turn words into numbers

      TF-IDF scores every word and two-word phrase: everyday words get low scores, distinctive ones get high scores. The article becomes a vector of weighted terms.

    3. 03

      Weigh the evidence

      Logistic regression has one learned weight per term. It adds up the weighted words in your text, converts the total into a probability, and we show the strongest words as your explanation.

    Read before you trust it

    This tool predicts based on writing patterns and is not a guarantee of truth. Always verify with trusted sources.
